Account Deletion

Delete your account

You can delete your Men's Group or Women's Group account at any time, directly in the app. Here is how, and exactly what is removed.

Deleting your account in the app

  1. Open Men's Group or Women's Group.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap the settings icon.
  4. Under Danger Zone, tap Delete account.
  5. Review what will be deleted, then tap Continue.
  6. Type your username to confirm, then tap Yes, delete.

What is permanently deleted

  • Your profile and goals
  • All host and join requests
  • Your login history
  • Your chat messages — deleted for everyone, in every conversation
  • Every event you organized — deleted, along with everyone's RSVPs to it

This cannot be undone. There is no grace period and no way to get your account back. Everything listed is deleted straight away.

Groups you host

A group you host goes to a co-host, or to another member if there is no co-host. If you are the only member, the group is deleted. Other members' accounts are not affected.

If you cannot access the app

Email us from the address your account is registered under:

support@socialnexus.group

Accounts deleted this way are removed within 30 days of the request.

Information we may keep

When you delete your account we delete or anonymize your personal information, unless we are required to retain it for legal or legitimate business purposes. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information in full.
